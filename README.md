# Mine-imator Bandrek Build

Mine-imator Bandrek Build ("Bandrek") is Stegripe's take on Mine-imator. It works like Mine-imator, with the same scenes, timeline and renderer. It adds real-time co-op editing, video and GIF support, and animation and visual tools requested by the Stegripe community.

| Layer | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Mine-imator** | The original animation tool by [David Andrei](https://www.mineimator.com), MIT licensed |
| **Simply Upscaled Build** | [Community fork](https://github.com/YogaindoCR/Mine-imator-2.0.2-Simply-Upscaled-Build) with upscaled assets and quality-of-life fixes |
| **Bandrek Build** | Stegripe's fork of Simply Upscaled Build |

## What Bandrek adds

- **Co-op**: open a party, share the code, and edit the same project together.
- **Video and GIF**: video clips on the timeline, video and GIF files as textures, and GIF export.
- **Animation tools**: a keyframe library for poses and animations, NLA strips, bend IK on every bendable part, spatial curves, flip pose and a keyframe speed adjuster.
- **Visuals**: color curves, a halftone and dither camera effect, more blend modes, and text with adjustable spacing and outline.
- **Tools**: an eyedropper and a teleporter for imported worlds.
- **Other**: Discord Rich Presence, an in-app updater, a new loading screen and double-click to open projects.

Bandrek is free and every vanilla Mine-imator feature works without an account. The Bandrek additions need full access, which is also free. To get it, log in with your Stegripe account, join the Bandrek Build Discord server and wear the Stegripe guild tag. See [Full access](https://bandrek.stegripe.org/docs/guides/access).

## Download

Get the latest Windows build, portable `.zip` or installer, from [bandrek.stegripe.org/downloads](https://bandrek.stegripe.org/downloads). Documentation lives at [bandrek.stegripe.org/docs](https://bandrek.stegripe.org/docs).

Only official Stegripe builds are supported. Modified binaries, forged `key.midata`, or redistributed “cracked” copies are unsupported and will not get Full access, co-op, or watermark-free export. Earned Full can keep a short signed offline ticket (at most 24 hours); without it, Full does not continue offline.

## This repository

Bandrek's own changes are developed privately by Stegripe. This repository holds the upstream Simply Upscaled Build source and is the place for bug reports and feature requests: open an [issue](https://github.com/stegripe/Mine-imator-Bandrek-Build/issues).

## Data policy

Your projects stay on your computer unless you open them in a co-op party. The app goes online for Mine-imator's own asset and news check, the Bandrek splash art and update check, your Stegripe login and access checks, and co-op sessions. For your login, Stegripe keeps your Discord user ID, your access time and a hash of the login token tied to a hashed fingerprint of your computer. Co-op data passes through the Stegripe relay between party members and is not saved.

Full detail: [Docs/DATA_POLICY.md](Docs/DATA_POLICY.md)
